01 / Workflow infrastructure

CausalOps

Auditable AI decision & rollout workflows

An AI proposal should be reviewable before it can become an action. I led the architecture and AI-assisted implementation of a prototype that turns business actions and operating constraints into evidence-linked experiment proposals and rollout contracts.

Typed claims and dependency checks separate model suggestions from execution authority. Immutable lifecycle records, storage boundaries, signed approvals, and targeted mutation tests make critical gates inspectable.
